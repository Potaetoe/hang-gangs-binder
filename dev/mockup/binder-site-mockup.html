Publish

Sends the charts above — as counts and medians, with no handles and no rows — to the members-only Progress page. It replaces whatever is there now.

Published as “Person 1”, “Person 2” — never handles — and each point is rounded to a date and a histogram bin, so following one person across two snapshots is an inference rather than a join. It is not impossible: anyone who knows what someone weighs may recognize their line, and publishing twice still discloses more than publishing once. Fewer than five lines publishes no chart at all. Off by default for those reasons.

Before you close this

This page is the only place the submissions exist in the clear. Closing the tab discards everything decrypted here, and the admin session is tab-scoped too — but the key is not. Once you import it, it stays on this device so later exports need no file, and Clear is what removes it. Press Clear before you leave this browser on a machine someone else can reach. Keep your key file as well: the browser may drop stored data, and the file is what puts the key back.

Faithful to the accounts branch at b330521 — markup, copy, tokens and component rules are lifted from apps/web directly. Two departures a mockup cannot avoid: the live site serves its vendored Playfair Display / DM Sans / JetBrains Mono woff2 files (this page shows the site's own fallback stacks), and the Telegram widget here is a drawn stand-in for the real iframe. Palette selection follows the site's own rule: Midnight is the default; a light-preferring system gets Daylight; a system asking for more contrast gets Contrast.

Shown ahead of the branch — issue #187: by owner direction, Sign in has left the rail (when signed out, the rail's session block is what offers the way back in), and the Theme control has moved from the rail to each page's footer — the pattern the sign-in page already carries. The branch at b330521 still shows both in the rail; #187 is the ticket that lands this.

Naming, ruled — issue #191: the owner walked three candidate schemes surface by surface and ruled the blend shown by default. The wordmark and site name are Hang Gang Binder — the binder is the gang's, and Muse's name sits on what she does, not over the door. The form page is Your page (you get a page in the shared binder — the sign-in copy already said so); the dashboard is Muse's charts, opening "Getting heavier? Muse certainly hopes so."; the tabs are On record / Weigh in; the rail note under Admin is removed outright ("seems silly to tell anyone what they may need"); Admin, Optional, Add to the binder, Received and the 404 stay as built. Three rules ride along: the door does not joke (Sign in / Sign out stay literal), the trust language does not joke (encryption, key, publish and clear copy stays plain), and the data does not joke (stat labels and units stay literal).
