Status

Not open yet. The form is still being built.

When it opens you will be asked for your Telegram username, your weight and your height, in whichever units you think in. Gender, roles and country are optional.

How your entry is handled

Your submission is encrypted in your browser before it is sent, so the service storing it cannot read it. Only the person holding the key can — and that key is not kept online.
